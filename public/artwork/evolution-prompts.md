# Evolution portrait atlases

The Ranger and Rick/Morty atlases were created with the built-in imagegen tool for this personal comic card game. The tool rejected the Marvel, DC and villain atlas requests; those files are not shipped, and those cards retain their original artwork with the existing evolution frame.

Each atlas is a square, exactly four columns by four rows, without gutters, text or borders. Portraits are centered waist-up with recognizable character identities, bold comic ink and rich colours. Cells 1–8 contain evolution I: upgraded armour and restrained thematic energy. Cells 9–16 contain evolution II of the same characters: elaborate armour and stronger energy. Mighty Morphin Rangers retain their suits and helmets with gold chest armour; Rick and Morty characters retain their animated identities with portal technology gear.

The exact ordered subjects requested per atlas:

- `evolution-rangers.png`: Red Ranger, Blue Ranger, Pink Ranger, Black Ranger, Yellow Ranger, White Ranger, Green Ranger, Lord Zedd.
- `evolution-marvel.png`: Spider-Man, Iron Man, Captain America, Black Widow, Wolverine, Star-Lord, Thor, Thanos.
- `evolution-dc.png`: Batman, The Flash, Wonder Woman, Nightwing, Raven, Aquaman, Superman, Darkseid.
- `evolution-rick.png`: Morty Smith, Summer Smith, Beth Smith, Jerry Smith, Evil Morty, Birdperson, Rick Sanchez, Phoenixperson.
- `evolution-villains.png`: Rita Repulsa, Goldar, Putty Patrol, Green Goblin, Ultron, The Joker, Harley Quinn, Putty Patrol commander. The eighth portrait is padding and is unused in the game.

These are imaginative game forms, not assertions of canonical costume designs. Original character artwork remains in use at card levels 1–4.
